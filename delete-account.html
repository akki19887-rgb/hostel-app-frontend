Delete Your Account

HostelOM · published by HOSTEL OM-F, Raipur, Chhattisgarh · Helpline +91 91832 06742

This page explains how to delete your HostelOM account (the app published on Google Play by HOSTEL OM-F), what is erased when you do, and what we are required to keep.

How to request deletion

From inside the app — takes under a minute

  1. Open the HostelOM app and sign in with the account you want to delete.
  2. On your dashboard, tap ⚙️ Account at the top right.
  3. Under Delete my account, type the word DELETE in the box.
  4. Tap the red Delete my account button. The account is closed immediately and you are signed out.

By email or phone

You can also ask us directly, without opening the app:

  • Email hostelomhoom@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject Delete my account.
  • Or call or WhatsApp the helpline on +91 91832 06742.

Tell us the mobile number or email address you signed up with. We will confirm the request with you and close the account within 7 working days.

You cannot delete while a stay is running. If you have a booking that is awaiting payment, paid, or checked in, the app will refuse the deletion and name the booking. Complete or cancel it first. Deleting your account does not cancel a booking and does not by itself create a refund — refunds follow our Refund Policy.

What is erased

All of this is deleted straight away, permanently, and cannot be recovered:

DataWhat happens
Your sign-in account — Google, mobile OTP, or email and passwordDeleted
Your profile: name, email address, mobile numberDeleted
Your ID documents: Aadhaar photo front and back, and your signed registration PDFDeleted from storage, file by file
Your guest registration recordsDeleted
Your messages to hostel ownersEmptied — the text is removed and your identity is unlinked
Your notification token, so the app stops reaching your phoneDeleted

What stays, and why

WhatWhyIn what form
Your past bookings The hostel needs its own record of who stayed, and tax law requires the transaction to be kept Kept, but stripped of you: the name becomes “Deleted user”, and the phone number, email and ID reference are emptied. It can no longer be traced back to you through the app.
Payment and invoice records Income Tax Act and GST rules 8 years from the end of that financial year
A one-line note that a deletion happened So we can prove to a regulator that we acted on your request The date and the old account number only — no name, no contact details
The other person's side of a chat It is the hostel owner's record of what was agreed Their own messages only. Yours are emptied.

Nothing else is kept. We do not keep a shadow copy of your profile, and we do not sell or share your data with advertisers.

Deletion is permanent. We cannot restore a deleted account, its bookings or its chat history. If you sign up again later it will be a fresh, empty account.
