3. How payment works, and what that means for your data

The guest pays twenty percent (20%) of the booking value on the Platform as HostelOM's booking and facilitation fee. The remaining eighty percent (80%) is paid directly to the hostel at check-in and never passes through us.

At present the 20% is paid by a direct UPI transfer that you initiate from your own banking or UPI application. We record only the transaction reference number (UTR) that you provide, so that we can match the payment to your booking. We do not receive, see or store any credential from your bank or UPI app.

When a payment gateway is introduced, it will be one licensed by the Reserve Bank of India, and card and banking details will be handled by that gateway under its own security standards — not by us.

4. Why we use your data, and on what basis

Under Section 6 of the DPDP Act we process your personal data on the basis of the consent you give when you create an account and when you make a booking. We use it only for these purposes:

  • To create and operate your account
  • To confirm, hold and manage your reservation
  • To pass the check-in details and identity document to the hostel you have booked, so that it can complete its statutory guest register
  • To contact you about your booking by SMS, WhatsApp, email or in-app notification
  • To verify payment and maintain accounting records
  • To prevent fraud, misuse and double-booking
  • To answer your complaints and comply with law

We do not use your personal data for advertising, profiling or any purpose you have not been told about here.

5. Identity documents — read this carefully

A hostel or paying-guest establishment in Chhattisgarh is required to keep an identity record of every resident. That obligation sits on the hostel, not on us; but the document is collected through the Platform so that your check-in is not delayed.

5.1 Which document to give

We ask you to prefer Voter ID, Driving Licence or Passport. If you choose Aadhaar, please upload a masked Aadhaar — the version in which the first eight digits are hidden. We do not require, and ask you not to upload, an unmasked Aadhaar.

5.2 Your consent at the point of upload

Before you upload, the Platform shows you this consent, and you give it voluntarily:

"I am voluntarily providing my identity document for verification and for the hostel's statutory record. I consent to HOSTEL OM-F processing it and sharing it with the hostel partner I have chosen. I understand it will be deleted 30 days after my check-out."

5.3 Where it is stored

  • The image is held in private cloud storage. It is not publicly accessible and carries no shareable link.
  • Only the hostel you booked, and authorised HostelOM staff, can view it. Every viewing is recorded in an audit log.
  • Our support staff cannot see your identity document at all, whatever their role.
  • Only the document type and its last four digits are written to the booking record.

6. How we keep it safe

We follow reasonable security practices and procedures as required by Section 43A of the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under it:

  • All traffic between your device and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Data at rest is held on Google Cloud infrastructure with encryption enabled
  • Access is granted by role. A staff member sees only what their role requires, and never identity documents or bank details
  • Every sensitive action — revealing a phone number, viewing a record, deleting an account — is written to an audit log with the identity of the person who did it
  • Staff sessions log out automatically after 30 minutes of inactivity
  • Owner bank and payout details are stored separately from public listing data

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and every affected user, as required by Section 8(6) of the DPDP Act.

8. Age — the Platform is for adults

HostelOM is intended only for persons aged eighteen (18) years or above. We do not knowingly collect the personal data of a child.

The Platform asks for your date of birth and refuses the booking if it shows you are under 18. Section 9 of the DPDP Act requires verifiable parental consent before a child's data may be processed, and a tick-box declaration is not verifiable consent.

If a booking is needed for someone under 18, a parent or lawful guardian must contact us on +91 91832 06742. We will arrange it with the hostel directly, with the guardian's own identity and consent on record.

If we learn that we hold the data of a person under 18 given without such consent, we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Under Sections 11 to 14 of the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Know what personal data of yours we hold and what we have done with it
  • Have it corrected, completed or updated
  • Have it erased, where we no longer need it for the purpose you gave it for
  • Withdraw your consent at any time — as easily as you gave it
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or become incapacitated
  • Have your grievance heard, and escalate to the Data Protection Board of India if we do not resolve it

Write to grievance@ho-om.in or call +91 91832 06742. We acknowledge within 24 to 48 hours and resolve within 15 business days.

Withdrawing consent does not undo what was lawfully done before, and it may mean we can no longer provide the booking service.

10. Deleting your account

You may delete your account and your data at any time, by either route:

  • In the app: Account › Delete my account. You will be asked to type DELETE to confirm.
  • By writing to support@ho-om.in from your registered email or phone. We complete it within 7 business days.

What deletion does

  • Your identity document images and registration forms are permanently deleted
  • Your profile, sign-in and push notification tokens are deleted
  • Records of completed stays are retained in de-identified form — your name and contact details are removed from them — for accommodation records, dispute resolution and statutory compliance
  • An account with a booking still in progress cannot be deleted until that booking is completed or cancelled. The app will tell you which booking is blocking it
