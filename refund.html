Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective Date: 24 September 2026 · Platform: ho-om.in and the HostelOM mobile application

1. Facilitation Fee

HostelOM is an online booking marketplace that connects guests with hostels and paying-guest accommodations. To confirm a reservation on the Platform, the guest pays an advance of twenty percent (20%) of the total booking value.

This 20% advance is HostelOM's technology and booking facilitation fee. It is earned by HostelOM at the moment the bed is reserved, because the bed is withdrawn from availability and held for that guest.

2. Cancellation — the advance is non-refundable

The 20% advance facilitation fee is strictly non-refundable in the event of:

  • cancellation by the guest, at any notice period, on any stay plan — daily, weekly or monthly;
  • a change of plan, a change of date, or late arrival;
  • the guest not turning up at all (no-show).

Cancelling a booking releases the reserved bed so that other guests may book it. It does not create any entitlement to a refund of the advance.

3. The remaining 80%

The remaining eighty percent (80%) of the booking value is paid by the guest directly to the Property Partner at the premises, at the time of physical check-in. HostelOM does not collect, hold, receive or remit this amount at any stage.

Any refund, adjustment or security deposit relating to that 80% is governed solely by the individual policy of the Property Partner. HostelOM is not a party to it and has no liability in respect of it.

4. When a full refund IS given

The advance is charged for holding a bed. Where that bed was not in fact made available, the fee was not earned, and the guest receives a refund of one hundred percent (100%) of the advance:

  • The Property Partner cancels the confirmed booking before check-in.
  • The guest arrives within the agreed check-in window and the bed that was paid for is not available — the premises are closed, the bed has been given to another person, or the booking has been double-allotted.

To claim under this clause the guest must contact HostelOM support on +91 91832 06742 before accepting any alternative bed or accommodation at the premises.

5. Refund timeline

Where a refund is approved under Clause 4, it will be initiated within forty-eight (48) hours of approval. Subject to standard banking timelines, the amount will be credited to the original account or UPI handle within five to seven (5–7) working days.

6. How to raise a refund request

  • Helpline (voice and WhatsApp): +91 91832 06742
  • Email: support@ho-om.in
  • Grievance Officer: grievance@ho-om.in

Please quote your Booking ID. Complaints are acknowledged within 24 to 48 hours and resolved within 15 business days.
