Tester setup — takes about 2 minutes

Why the app isn't showing up yet

HostelOM is in closed testing on Google Play. It is invisible on the Play Store until you accept the tester invitation once. The normal Play Store link will keep saying “Item not found” until that is done — that is expected, nothing is broken.

Use the button below. Do not search for “HostelOM” in the Play Store — it will not appear there.

Do these 3 steps in order

  1. Open this page in Chrome

    If you tapped the link inside WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook, you are in their mini-browser, which is not signed in to Google — the next step will fail there. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top corner and choose “Open in Chrome” (or “Open in browser”).

  2. Accept the invitation Become a tester →

    On that page tap “Become a tester”. You should then see “You are a tester”. Make sure the Google account shown at the top right is the same Gmail you gave us.

  3. Download the app Open HostelOM on Play Store →

    This link only works after step 2. If it still says “Item not found”, wait 5 minutes, then pull down to refresh the Play Store page. The app page will say (Early access) — that is correct.

Still stuck? Find your case

“Item not found” even after becoming a tester
Almost always the Play Store app is signed in with a different Google account than the one that accepted the invitation. Open the Play Store → tap your photo in the top right → check the email shown. If it is not the Gmail you gave us, switch accounts, then open the tester link again. Google can also take up to 10 minutes to sync — clearing the Play Store cache (Settings → Apps → Google Play Store → Storage → Clear cache) makes it instant.
The tester page won't load, or asks me to sign in again and again
You are in an in-app browser. Copy this address and paste it into Chrome directly:
play.google.com/apps/testing/in.ho_om.twa
It says “This testing program is full” or “not eligible”
Your Gmail is not on the tester list — usually a small spelling difference (an extra letter, or a different address than the one on your phone). Send us the exact Gmail shown in your Play Store account and we will add it. There is no limit problem on our side.
I don't want to install anything — can I just see it?
Yes. HostelOM runs in the browser too, with every feature the app has: Open ho-om.in in the browser → Testing through the Play Store app still helps us a lot, because that is what Google counts.
I'm on an iPhone
The Play Store app is Android-only. On iPhone, open ho-om.in in Safari, then tap Share → Add to Home Screen — it behaves like an app.

What to do once it opens

Please just use it normally for a few minutes — sign in, search for a hostel or PG in Raipur, open a listing, look at the photos and the map. Anything that looks wrong, slow or confusing is worth telling us, however small.

Keep the app installed for the next few days, even if you don't open it daily. Google requires testers to stay opted in for 14 continuous days — uninstalling resets the clock for everyone.

Report anything to hostelomhoom@gmail.com or reply on WhatsApp.
